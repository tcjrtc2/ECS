Our Services

We offer Intensive In-Home Services aimed at enhancing family functioning and addressing significant impairments in major life activities. Common issues addressed include, but are not limited to:

  • Anger Management
  • Anxiety and Depression
  • Alcohol and Drug Abuse
  • Child Discipline Techniques
  • Interpersonal Conflicts
  • Delinquent Behaviors
  • Grief and Loss
  • School Attendance
  • Parent/Child Conflicts

Intensive In-Home Services

  • These services aim to enhance family functioning and address significant mental impairments.
  • Services are usually time-limited, provided in the residence of an at-risk individual.
  • Individuals must demonstrate significant impairments impacting major life activities.
  • Includes crisis treatment, individual/family counseling, skills training, case management, 24/7 response.
