About Us

“We recognize and understand that focusing on mental health is necessary in addressing the overall well-being of individuals in our community.”

Exquisite Counseling Services is licensed by the Virginia Department of Behavioral Health and Developmental Services (DBHDS). Our team of highly trained professionals is dedicated to delivering exceptional care and support to individuals in need. Our services are funded through Medicaid, FAPT, CSA, grants, and self-pay options are also available.
