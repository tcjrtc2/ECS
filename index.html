What is Intensive In-Home Services?

Intensive In-Home Services are interventions for children and adolescents who have, or are at risk of, serious behavioral or emotional disturbances, which often include a mental health diagnosis. We provide Intensive In-Home Services to individuals ages 4 through 21.

IIHS Services Include

  • Individual and family counseling
  • Parenting and communication skills training
  • Case management and coordination with other providers
  • 24-hour Crisis Response
  • Family communication
  • Behavior Management
  • Interpersonal Skills
  • Coping Skills

Eligibility Criteria

  • Individuals at risk of out-of-home placement, or those transitioning back home.
  • Demonstrating significant impairments affecting major life activities or inability to recognize danger.
  • Have a documented history of repeated interventions for inappropriate behaviors.
  • Various types of trauma
  • Be between the ages of 4 and 21.

Common Issues Addressed

  • Anger management
  • Anxiety and depression
  • Alcohol and drug abuse
  • Child discipline techniques
  • Interpersonal conflicts
  • Delinquent behaviors (e.g., violence, gang activity)
  • Grief and loss
  • School attendance issues
  • Parent/child conflicts
  • Poor communication skills
  • Bullying and aggression
