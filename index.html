Key Facts: Adolescent Mental Health

  • • Internationally, approximately 1 in 7 individuals aged 10–19 suffer from a mental disorder.
  • • Mental disorders account for 15% of the overall burden of disease in this age group.
  • • Depression, anxiety, and behavioral disorders are leading causes of illness and disability.
  • • Suicide is the third leading cause of death among 15–29 year olds.
  • • Early intervention is critical to prevent long-term physical, emotional, and social challenges.

What is Intensive In-Home Services?

Intensive In-Home Services (IIHS) are family preservation interventions for children and adolescents who have, or are at risk of, serious behavioral or emotional disturbances—often including a mental health diagnosis. We provide IIHS to individuals ages 4 through 21.

IIHS Services Include:

  • • Crisis treatment
  • • Individual and family counseling
  • • Parenting and communication skills training
  • • Case management and coordination with other providers
  • • 24/7 emergency response

Eligibility Criteria

IIHS is typically provided in the home of individuals at risk of out-of-home placement, or those transitioning back home. To qualify, individuals must:

  • • Demonstrate significant impairments affecting major life activities or inability to recognize danger.
  • • Have a documented history of repeated interventions for inappropriate behaviors.
  • • Be between the ages of 4 and 21.

Common Issues Addressed

  • Interpersonal conflicts
  • Delinquent behaviors (e.g., violence, gang activity)
